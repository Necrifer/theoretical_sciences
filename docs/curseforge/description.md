# Theoretical Sciences

**An endgame tech mod at the edge of theoretical physics.**

Entangle qubits, trap antimatter, condense Bose-Einstein matter, forge micro black holes, fold space into tesseracts,
harvest the quantum foam, prove theorems to unlock machines — and at the end of everything, take apart and rebuild the
Omnipotence Core itself.

Progression runs through six tiers, from a first Casimir press to the Transcendental. Every step is a machine you
tune, a theory you derive, or a world you have to survive.

---

## Machines that run on physics

* **78 machines, 37 of them multiblocks.**
* The great multiblocks run on **real physics models**. The operator sets beam energies, field strengths and
  temperatures on a console, and each recipe needs its quantities held inside a window — a collider needs its
  centre-of-mass energy, a plasma crucible its confinement.
* Tuned multiblocks **drift, wear their containment, overclock and run parallel recipes**. Coils and field emitters
  come in tiers.
* Let containment fail and the machine releases its hazard: fractured reality, nanite swarms, paradox echoes, law
  wraiths.
* The late-game machines open a full **operator console**: a live simulation of the recipe, an oscilloscope of the
  quantity it must hold, a terminal log and the program the console runs.

## Research that has to be understood

* A research tree of **148 entries**, paid for with Insight from observation and experiment.
* Many entries are unlocked by solving a **derivation puzzle**: build the equation from its symbols, then find the
  answer. The given values differ in every world, so the answers can't be looked up.

## Ten dimensions

The **Quantum Foam**, the **Bulk** between branes, **Hyperdimensional Reality**, the **Platonic Realm**, the
**Transfinite Hierarchy**, the **Antiverse**, the interiors of **black and white holes**, the **Living Proof** and the
**Transcendental**. Each has its own laws, creatures, materials and soundtrack. Near their impossible structures,
space itself folds on your screen.

## The end of everything

* The Omnilex Engine's impossible recipe opens the **Transcendental**.
* **Alpha Omega** waits in the Living Proof.
* The **Omnipotence Core** can materialize and forge anything — until the Shutdown Sequence, the Sundering and the
  Rekindling.
* After that come weapons, tools and armour that run on **Eschaton Charge**. Last of all comes an Omnipotence Core of
  your own, which has to be attuned to every place it stands in.

## And more

* **Singularities for every mod:** every ingot of every installed mod gets one automatically.
* **The Quantum Codex**, the mod's own guide book: its maps are grouped into the six tiers, it reveals itself as you
  progress, and it shows how to make everything in it.
* **874 items and blocks, 48 fluids, 25 creatures**, with an original synthesised soundtrack, sound effects and
  particles of its own.

## Compatibility

* **No required dependencies.** No mixins, no coremods.
* Standard **FE**, item and fluid capabilities on every machine, and **Interface Ports** for automation.
* **JEI** or **EMI**: every recipe, plus rotating 3D structure previews.
* **Jade**: look at a machine to see its status, the recipe it is making and a progress bar with the time left.
* **AE2**: Interface Ports join an ME network.
* **KubeJS**: recipes, research, machine numbers, structure tiers, Forge recipes, singularities and events —
  see the customisation guide below.

## For modpack developers

Almost everything is data, changeable with a **plain datapack** or with **KubeJS**: machine recipes, the research
tree, machine numbers, new coil and emitter tiers, physics models, the Omnipotence Core's Forge, singularities and the
tags the mod reads.

The full guides, with examples, are in the source repository: **docs/DATAPACKS.md** and **docs/KUBEJS.md**.
